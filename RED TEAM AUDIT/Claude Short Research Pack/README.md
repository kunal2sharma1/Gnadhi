# Claude Short Research Pack

Purpose: use six Claude accounts during the five-hour cooldown for narrow, independent research questions that can materially improve the project's later decisions.

Rules:
- Each account gets exactly one packet.
- Work independently; do not read other worker outputs.
- Do not modify main project decision files.
- Save the completed research directly to the assigned output file.
- Preserve source links and distinguish source-derived fact, scholarly interpretation, and inference.
- Focus on the assigned question only; do not expand into a general Gandhi research project.
- A negative finding is valuable. Do not force a positive answer.
- These results are evidence inputs for later PM synthesis, not automatic decisions.
