# Claude Research 5 — Godse Counter-Memory Verification

## Research Question
How strong is the documentary evidence for a distinct post-1948 Gandhi–Godse counter-memory, and does it justify a core chapter?

## Scope
Trace the post-assassination memory relationship between Gandhi and Godse.
Research:
- Godse's trial statement and later writings;
- post-1948 representations of Godse;
- commemorative activity around Godse;
- publications, organisations, memorial practices, speeches and political references;
- explicit invocations of Godse in later decades;
- cases where Gandhi and Godse are positioned as competing symbols.

Distinguish:
1. Godse's own memory project;
2. private admiration;
3. organised counter-memory;
4. fringe commemoration;
5. mainstream political invocation;
6. contemporary individual statements;
7. proven organisational continuity.

## Required Output
1. Timeline of post-1948 counter-memory evidence.
2. Strongest primary sources.
3. Strongest scholarly interpretations.
4. Evidence of continuity vs isolated recurrence.
5. Evidence weakening the continuity argument.
6. Whether this is genuinely assassination-specific in a way Patel/Nehru comparisons cannot reproduce.
7. Whether Chapter 14 should remain core, be narrowed, or be merged.

## Rules
Do not generalise from isolated political remarks. Do not infer organisational responsibility from individual praise. Preserve exact source links and dates.
