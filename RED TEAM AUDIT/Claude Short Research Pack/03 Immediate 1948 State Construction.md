# Claude Research 3 — Immediate 1948 State Construction

## Research Question
What did the Indian state and political leadership actually do in the days/weeks after Gandhi's assassination to construct meanings of Gandhi, and which parts were genuinely assassination-specific?

## Scope
Focus tightly on 30 January–February 1948 and, where necessary, the immediate funeral/ash period.
Research:
- Nehru broadcast;
- Cabinet discussions;
- official government resolutions;
- communal-organisation/security measures;
- funeral and mourning rituals;
- Gandhi's ashes and memorialisation;
- language used to define what Gandhi 'stood for'.

Distinguish:
1. mourning;
2. political shock;
3. security/state response;
4. explicit selection of Gandhi's meanings;
5. broader nation-building processes already underway.

## Required Output
1. Chronological evidence table with exact date and source.
2. What the state explicitly said Gandhi represented.
3. What policy actions followed.
4. Which actions can plausibly be linked specifically to the assassination.
5. Which were already underway before 30 January.
6. Best scholarship interpreting the evidence.
7. Evidence that complicates or weakens the state-construction argument.
8. Clear distinction between state action and later retrospective interpretation.

## Rules
Prefer primary government/archival records and peer-reviewed scholarship. Do not generalise from one speech or one resolution. Preserve exact source links.
