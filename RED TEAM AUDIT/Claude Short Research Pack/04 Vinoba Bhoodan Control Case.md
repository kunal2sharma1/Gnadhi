# Claude Research 4 — Vinoba/Bhoodan as Control Case

## Research Question
Does Vinoba Bhave's Bhoodan-Gramdan/Sarvodaya inheritance from Gandhi actually depend on Gandhi's assassination, or does it provide a natural-death-resistant continuity case?

## Scope
Trace the transmission chain:
Gandhi → Vinoba → Sarvodaya/Bhoodan-Gramdan.

Test:
- Vinoba's pre-1948 relationship with Gandhi;
- Gandhi's constructive programme;
- post-1948 Sarvodaya formation;
- Bhoodan-Gramdan principles and practice;
- whether Vinoba explicitly referenced Gandhi's death as changing his programme;
- whether Gandhi's living supervision was important to the movement's form;
- what would plausibly continue if Gandhi had died naturally at the same point.

## Required Output
1. Transmission timeline.
2. Evidence for pre-1948 inheritance.
3. Evidence for post-1948 change caused by death/assassination.
4. Evidence for independence from assassination.
5. What Gandhi could have corrected/revised if alive.
6. Strongest argument for treating Chapter 11 as a control/contrast case.
7. Strongest argument against that treatment.
8. Recommended chapter function.
9. Primary-source and scholarly links.

## Rules
Do not decide the chapter's survival merely from the amount of evidence. The question is causal relevance to the assassination thesis.
