# Claude Research 6 — Selective Reclamation and Modern Gandhi Use

## Research Question
What can be demonstrated about post-1948 selective reclamation of Gandhi by political actors, and which contemporary cases actually deepen the historical argument?

## Scope A — Chapter 13
Investigate Deendayal Upadhyaya / Integral Humanism / Bharatiya Jana Sangh.
Test:
- explicit references to Gandhi;
- engagement with Hind Swaraj and Gandhian economic thought;
- what was adopted;
- what was changed;
- what was rejected;
- ideological differences;
- whether 'reclamation', 'adaptation', 'repurposing', or 'appropriation' is the most defensible term;
- whether evidence suggests sincere intellectual engagement or strategic use.

## Scope B — Contemporary Test
Screen a small number of modern cases, including where useful:
- Swachh Bharat;
- NCERT textbook changes;
- official Gandhi commemoration;
- contemporary Gandhi/Godse controversies.

For each case determine whether it is:
CONTINUITY / INHERITANCE / APPROPRIATION / COUNTER-MEMORY / RECURRENCE / ANALOGY / UNRELATED.

## Required Output
1. Chapter 13 evidence table.
2. Strongest contrary evidence.
3. Best terminology and why.
4. Modern case comparison table.
5. Which modern case provides genuine explanatory value rather than mere contemporary relevance.
6. Whether Chapter 13 and Chapter 15 should remain separate.
7. Primary sources and serious scholarship with links.

## Rules
Do not infer motive from use. Do not call a modern case continuity without documented transmission. Negative findings are valuable.
