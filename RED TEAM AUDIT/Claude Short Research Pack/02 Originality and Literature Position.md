# Claude Research 2 — Originality and Literature Position

## Research Question
How much of the current book argument has already been substantially argued in existing scholarship, and what defensible contribution remains?

## Scope
Search serious scholarship on:
- Gandhi's political afterlife / legacy;
- Gandhi assassination and post-assassination politics;
- mourning, ritual, secular nationalism, and state consolidation;
- Gandhi and Hindu nationalism/RSS;
- Godse and political violence;
- Gandhian inheritance / Sarvodaya;
- Gandhi's selective reclamation by later political actors.

Specifically test the territory represented by Yasmin Khan, Mira Debs, Ashis Nandy, Claude Markovits, A.G. Noorani, Christophe Jaffrelot, and the recent literature on Gandhi/RSS/Golwalkar. Find additional important works.

## Required Output
1. Literature map by theme.
2. Claims that are already well established.
3. Claims that are adjacent but not identical to the project.
4. What appears genuinely under-synthesised.
5. Whether the book should claim a new argument, new synthesis, new evidence, or primarily a new comparative structure.
6. Five most important works the eventual book must directly engage.
7. Recommended originality statement that does not overclaim.

## Rules
Use serious scholarly sources. Do not call the project original merely because no single book contains every element. Include publication details and links.
