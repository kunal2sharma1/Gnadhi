# Claude Research 1 — Natural-Death Comparison

## Research Question
What parts of Gandhi's posthumous political inheritance can be shown to be assassination-specific, rather than ordinary consequences of the death of a major political founder?

## Scope
Compare Gandhi with Sardar Patel (natural death, 1950) and Jawaharlal Nehru (natural death, 1964), focusing on:
- posthumous contest over political legacy;
- state commemoration/canonisation;
- political appropriation or reclamation;
- competing interpretations;
- memorialisation;
- later partisan use.

Then isolate what Gandhi has that Patel/Nehru do not, especially:
- assassin-specific counter-memory;
- Godse's stated ideological reasoning;
- recurring later invocation of Godse;
- immediate assassination-linked political/security consequences.

## Required Output
1. Evidence table: Gandhi / Patel / Nehru.
2. Which mechanisms are death-general.
3. Which mechanisms are plausibly assassination-specific.
4. Which mechanisms are unique to Gandhi-Godse.
5. Strongest evidence against the current thesis.
6. Strongest evidence preserving an assassination-specific contribution.
7. Recommended language for the eventual thesis.
8. Primary sources + serious scholarship with links.

## Rules
Do not write a biography. Do not invent a counterfactual. A negative finding is valuable.
