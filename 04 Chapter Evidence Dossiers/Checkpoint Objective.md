# Checkpoint 4 — Chapter Evidence Dossiers

## Status
DESIGN COMPLETE — EXECUTION GATED BY RED-TEAM VERDICT

## Objective

Convert the approved chapter research specifications from Checkpoint 3 into chapter-level evidence dossiers containing the actual documentary and scholarly material required to support, challenge, narrow, merge, or remove each chapter.

This checkpoint is **not manuscript drafting**.

It is the bridge between:

research specification → verified evidence base → drafting readiness.

## Required outcome

For every chapter that survives the red-team and PM review, establish:

1. precise claims the chapter can safely make;
2. primary-source evidence for each major claim;
3. scholarly support and disagreement;
4. counterevidence;
5. source-quality assessment;
6. causal status of each claim;
7. unresolved gaps;
8. chapter admission / merge / remove decision;
9. a compact evidence dossier that later drafting workers can rely on.

## Mandatory gate

The unfinished overnight red-team audit must be completed before evidence-dossier execution begins.

The red-team may change:

- central concept;
- causal model;
- chapter count;
- chapter scope;
- chapter order;
- evidence priorities;
- worker allocation.

Therefore no large-scale Checkpoint 4 evidence collection should begin until its final verdict has been reviewed and adopted or explicitly rejected by the Product Manager.

## Evidence standard

Every load-bearing claim should be classified as:

- VERIFIED FACT
- ACTOR CLAIM
- LEGAL / INQUIRY FINDING
- SCHOLARLY INTERPRETATION
- PROJECT INFERENCE
- UNRESOLVED

Do not upgrade one category into another without evidence.

## Causal classification

Use a separate proximity axis:

- PRE-EXISTING
- DEATH-GENERAL
- ASSASSINATION-SPECIFIC
- STATE / NATION-BUILDING ERA
- LATER-INDEPENDENT

Use a separate evidence/inheritance axis where relevant:

- SYMBOLIC USE
- SELECTIVE USE
- SUBSTANTIVE INHERITANCE
- DOCUMENTED CONTINUITY

The two axes must never be collapsed.
