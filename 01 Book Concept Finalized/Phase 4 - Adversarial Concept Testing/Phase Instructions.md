# Phase 4 — Adversarial Concept Testing

## Objective
Try to break the working concept and thesis before the project invests heavily in historical research.

## What this phase must accomplish
Test whether the concept is coherent, historically responsible, and strong enough to support a serious book. Search for alternative explanations, unsupported assumptions, ambiguous causal claims, overreach, political bias, and places where the project could become advocacy instead of investigation.

## Sub-phases

### Sub-phase 4.1 — Strongest Counterargument
**Assigned to:** ChatGPT Go execution account — Adversarial role

**Work:** Read the complete supplied context from Phases 1–3. Build the strongest serious case against the current concept and working thesis. Attack the strongest version, not a weak version. Identify evidence that could genuinely defeat, weaken, or require major revision of the thesis.

**Output:** `04.1 Strongest Counterargument.md`

### Sub-phase 4.2 — Alternative Explanations and Framings
**Assigned to:** ChatGPT Go execution account

**Work:** Examine whether the observed problem could be explained differently. Propose alternative framings that preserve the author’s intellectual interest while reducing unsupported assumptions. Compare their strengths and weaknesses with the current thesis.

**Output:** `04.2 Alternative Explanations and Framings.md`

### Sub-phase 4.3 — Product Manager Concept Revision
**Assigned to:** This ChatGPT account — Product Manager

**Work:** Review the counterargument and alternative explanations. Decide what must change. Revise the working concept, central question, and thesis where necessary. Record every major change and its reason.

**Output:** `04.3 Revised Working Concept.md`

## Execution rule for all sub-phases
The Product Manager prepares a self-contained context pack for every execution account. No agent should need access to an earlier chat session. The only authority for project context is the supplied approved material.

## Handoff rule
The adversarial outputs must state:
- strongest objection
- evidence needed to evaluate it
- parts of the thesis that survive
- parts that fail or remain uncertain
- recommended changes

## Completion condition
The project either survives the adversarial test with stronger reasoning or has been materially revised to address the weaknesses discovered.

## Next phase dependency
Phase 5 must use the revised concept and the complete adversarial record to prepare the final working concept package and checkpoint review.
