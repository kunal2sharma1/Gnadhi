# Phase 1 — Capture the Original Concept

## Objective
Capture the author’s original idea for *The Final Walk of Glory* accurately before outside research or AI interpretation begins to reshape it.

## What this phase must accomplish
Create a clear record of what the author currently believes the book should investigate, why the idea matters, what emotional and intellectual effect the book should have, and what the author currently means by its central phrases.

The author’s raw thinking should be preserved even when the grammar is imperfect or the idea is incomplete. This phase is about capturing the idea, not proving it.

## Sub-phases

### Sub-phase 1.1 — Author Concept Interview
**Assigned to:** ChatGPT

**Work:** Ask focused questions and help the author explain the idea in his own words. Capture the intended subject, purpose, central conflict, desired reader experience, title meaning, and initial argument.

**Output:** `01.1 Author Concept Record.md`

### Sub-phase 1.2 — Concept Consolidation
**Assigned to:** ChatGPT

**Work:** Read the author concept record and convert it into a clear concept summary without adding historical claims that the author has not made or supported.

**Output:** `01.2 Concept Summary.md`

## Agent instructions
The assigned agent must read this phase file and all completed files in this phase before beginning work. Preserve the author’s underlying ideas. Do not turn assumptions into facts.

## Completion condition
The author’s original concept is clearly documented and can be read independently by another researcher.

## Next phase dependency
Phase 2 must use the completed concept record and concept summary as its starting context.
