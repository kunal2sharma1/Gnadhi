# Phase 5 — Cross-Worker Review and Targeted Gap Research

## Objective
Compare the three GPT workstreams, identify where they agree or disagree, and close only the research gaps that materially affect the concept.

## Sub-phases

### 5.1 — Cross-Worker Comparison
**Assigned to:** This ChatGPT account — Product Manager

Compare Worker 1, Worker 2, and Worker 3. Record agreements, disagreements, strongest reasoning, weak reasoning, and conclusions that require further evidence.

**Output:** `05.1 Cross-Worker Comparison.md`

### 5.2 — Gap Prioritisation
**Assigned to:** This ChatGPT account — Product Manager

Rank unresolved questions by importance. Separate questions that could change the thesis from questions that are merely interesting background.

**Output:** `05.2 Research Gap Priorities.md`

### 5.3 — Targeted Claude Gap Research
**Assigned to:** One or more Claude accounts as available

Only when a high-priority gap requires external research, create a sharply bounded Claude assignment. Never send a broad follow-up research request. Each assignment must answer one clearly defined missing question.

**Output:** `05.3 Targeted Gap Research Packet.md` or additional numbered packets.

### 5.4 — GPT Reconciliation Round
**Assigned to:** GPT Workers 1, 2, and 3 in parallel

Give each worker the relevant gap research and ask them to update only the parts of their analysis affected by the new evidence. They must explicitly state what changed.

**Outputs:** Updated worker analysis files.

## Completion condition
Major disagreements have been understood, material research gaps have been addressed where necessary, and the Product Manager has a stable evidence base for final synthesis.
