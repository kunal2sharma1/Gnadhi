# Claude Research Packet Standard

## Purpose
Every Claude research assignment must produce a focused research packet that can be used by ChatGPT workers without access to the original Claude conversation.

## Required sections

### Research question
State the exact question answered.

### Scope
State what was investigated.

### Exclusions
State what was deliberately not investigated.

### Key findings
List only findings directly relevant to the research question.

### Primary evidence
Identify original documents, statements, speeches, trial material, archival material, or other primary evidence used.

### Secondary evidence
Identify serious scholarly or historical works used.

### Important quotations
Include only quotations that can be accurately attributed and provide enough source detail for later verification.

### Competing interpretations
Show meaningful disagreement rather than forcing one conclusion.

### What the evidence supports
State the strongest claims that can safely be made.

### What the evidence does not support
State claims that would be too strong, misleading, or unsupported.

### Unresolved questions
List questions the research could not settle.

### Research gaps
Identify useful but genuinely missing evidence.

### Handoff to GPT
Explain exactly how the findings may be relevant to the current checkpoint and what should be treated cautiously.

## Workload rule
The assignment must remain narrow enough that one Claude free session can complete it without needing a five-hour cooldown before producing a usable result. Breadth is created across the seven Claude accounts, not inside one assignment.
