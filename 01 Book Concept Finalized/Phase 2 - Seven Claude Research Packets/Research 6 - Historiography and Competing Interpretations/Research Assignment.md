# Claude Research Assignment 6 — Historiography and Competing Interpretations

## Objective
Determine how serious historians and scholars interpret the assassination, its motives, and the meaning of Gandhi's legacy.

## Research question
Where do historians agree, where do they disagree, and what evidence explains the disagreement?

## Scope
Focus on reputable historical scholarship and major scholarly interpretations directly relevant to the book's concept.

## Exclusions
Do not create a general literature review of all Gandhi scholarship. Do not treat online political commentary as equivalent to historical scholarship.

## Required output
Return a Markdown Research Packet containing major interpretations, key scholars and works, evidence used by each interpretation, major disagreements, important quotations with source details, safe claims, unsafe claims, and unresolved historiographical questions.

## Important rule
Do not select the interpretation that best fits the book. Report the strongest competing interpretations fairly.
