# Claude Research Assignment 5 — Strong Criticisms of Gandhi

## Objective
Identify the strongest serious criticisms of Gandhi that could challenge the book's framing and prevent a one-sided treatment.

## Research question
Which major criticisms of Gandhi are directly relevant to the book's proposed argument, and what evidence supports them?

## Scope
Focus on major critics and scholarship on Gandhi's politics, communalism, caste, methods, political compromises, and other criticisms that could materially affect the book's interpretation.

## Exclusions
Do not produce a general catalogue of every criticism ever made of Gandhi. Do not turn the assignment into a biography of his critics.

## Required output
Provide a Markdown Research Packet with major critics, primary evidence where available, scholarly interpretations, important quotations, disputed points, strongest criticisms, strongest responses, safe claims, unsafe claims, and gaps.

## Important rule
The purpose is to strengthen the book by identifying legitimate criticism, not to defend or attack Gandhi in advance.
