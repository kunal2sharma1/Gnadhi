# Claude Research Assignment 3 — Ideological and Organisational Context

## Objective
Establish the relevant ideological and organisational context without collapsing distinct movements, organisations, or thinkers into one category.

## Research question
What are the documented relationships, similarities, differences, and boundaries among the political and ideological actors relevant to discussion of Gandhi's assassination?

## Scope
Focus on the distinctions and documented relationships among Savarkar, Hindu Mahasabha, RSS, and related currents only to the extent necessary to understand the book's initial concept.

## Exclusions
Do not provide a general history of Hindu nationalism. Do not attribute every action of one organisation to another. Do not use modern political labels as substitutes for historical evidence.

## Required output
Provide a Markdown Research Packet with primary evidence, serious scholarship, relevant quotations, competing interpretations, disputed relationships, safe claims, unsafe claims, and gaps.

## Important rule
Organisational affiliation, ideological influence, sympathy, political proximity, and direct involvement are different claims and must be treated separately.
