# Claude Research Assignment 1 — Assassination Facts and Immediate Record

## Objective
Establish the narrow historical record of Gandhi's assassination and its immediate aftermath that is relevant to assessing the book's concept.

## Research question
What can be established with high confidence about the assassination, and which details or interpretations are disputed?

## Scope
Focus on the documented events surrounding 30 January 1948, immediate reactions, primary evidence, trial-related material, and major scholarly disputes directly connected to the assassination.

## Exclusions
Do not write a full Gandhi biography. Do not research the entire history of Hindu nationalism. Do not attempt to explain the entire post-1948 legacy.

## Required output
Return a Markdown Research Packet containing: key findings, primary sources, secondary sources, important quotations with source details, disputed points, what can safely be claimed, what cannot safely be claimed, gaps, and handoff notes.

## Important rule
Separate documented facts from later interpretations. Do not infer ideological responsibility beyond what the evidence supports.
