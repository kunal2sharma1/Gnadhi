# Claude Research Assignment 7 — Gandhi's Legacy, Memory, and Modern Political Use

## Objective
Test the part of the book concept suggesting that the struggle over Gandhi continued after his death.

## Research question
How has Gandhi's image, ideas, memory, and reputation been defended, criticised, appropriated, neutralised, or contested after 1948?

## Scope
Focus on evidence of changing public, political, educational, cultural, and scholarly interpretations of Gandhi that are relevant to the book's central idea.

## Exclusions
Do not write a broad history of independent India. Do not assume that criticism of Gandhi equals an attempt to destroy Gandhi's legacy. Do not assume that political use of Gandhi means genuine agreement with his philosophy.

## Required output
Provide a Markdown Research Packet with major examples, relevant primary and secondary sources, competing interpretations, important quotations with source details, safe claims, unsafe claims, and research gaps.

## Important rule
Separate criticism, appropriation, symbolic use, historical reassessment, and deliberate attempts to discredit Gandhi. These are different phenomena and must not be merged without evidence.
