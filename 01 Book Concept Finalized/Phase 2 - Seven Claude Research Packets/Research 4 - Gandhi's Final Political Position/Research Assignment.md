# Claude Research Assignment 4 — Gandhi's Final Political Position

## Objective
Establish Gandhi's own political priorities and public interventions in his final period that are directly relevant to the book's concept.

## Research question
What was Gandhi actually arguing and doing in his final months on communal violence, Partition, inter-community relations, political responsibility, and nonviolence?

## Scope
Focus on Gandhi's final political activity, speeches, writings, fasts, meetings, and statements relevant to these questions.

## Exclusions
Do not write a full biography. Do not evaluate every part of Gandhi's philosophy. Do not research his entire career unless a short comparison is necessary.

## Required output
Return a Markdown Research Packet with primary evidence, source details, scholarly interpretation, important quotations, disagreements, safe claims, unsafe claims, and research gaps.

## Important rule
Prefer Gandhi's own words and contemporaneous evidence. Distinguish what Gandhi said from later claims about what he represented.
