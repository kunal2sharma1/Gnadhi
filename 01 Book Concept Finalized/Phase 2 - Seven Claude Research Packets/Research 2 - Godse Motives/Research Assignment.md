# Claude Research Assignment 2 — Godse's Motives and Ideological Position

## Objective
Establish what Nathuram Godse himself argued were his motives for killing Gandhi and how serious scholarship interprets those arguments.

## Research question
What did Godse say motivated him, what evidence exists for those motives, and how do historians assess his claims?

## Scope
Use Godse's own writings or statements where available, trial material, contemporaneous evidence, and serious historical scholarship.

## Exclusions
Do not generalise Godse's views to every Hindu nationalist, RSS member, Hindu Mahasabha member, or Indian nationalist. Do not research post-1948 political movements except where necessary to interpret Godse's stated motives.

## Required output
Provide a Markdown Research Packet with primary evidence, scholarly interpretations, quotations with source details, disputed points, safe claims, unsafe claims, and research gaps.

## Important rule
Keep individual responsibility, ideological influence, organisational connection, and later political interpretation as separate questions.
