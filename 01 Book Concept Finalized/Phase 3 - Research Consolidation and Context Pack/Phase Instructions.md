# Phase 3 — Research Consolidation and Context Pack

## Objective
Turn the seven Claude research packets into one reliable working context that the three GPT workers can use without needing to reconstruct the research themselves.

## What this phase must accomplish
Check the research packets for overlap, contradictions, missing evidence, and unsupported claims. Preserve useful disagreement instead of flattening it. Then create a concise Context Pack containing only the material needed for the parallel GPT work.

## Sub-phases

### 3.1 — Research Packet Comparison
**Assigned to:** GPT Worker 1

Compare all seven research packets. Identify agreement, contradiction, duplication, and important gaps.

**Output:** `03.1 Research Comparison.md`

### 3.2 — Research Reliability and Gap Review
**Assigned to:** GPT Worker 2

Independently audit the packets for weak evidence, unclear sourcing, overclaiming, missing perspectives, and questions that require additional research.

**Output:** `03.2 Research Reliability Review.md`

### 3.3 — Research Use Map
**Assigned to:** GPT Worker 3

Map which research findings are relevant to which conceptual questions. Identify which findings can inform the concept and which should remain background only.

**Output:** `03.3 Research Use Map.md`

### 3.4 — Master Research Context Pack
**Assigned to:** This ChatGPT account — Product Manager

Consolidate the three reviews and the seven original packets into one master context pack. Include source references, disagreements, risks, open questions, and the specific material each GPT workstream should use.

**Output:** `03.4 Master Research Context Pack.md`

## Completion condition
A clean, self-contained research context pack exists for the next phase, with unresolved disagreements explicitly preserved.
