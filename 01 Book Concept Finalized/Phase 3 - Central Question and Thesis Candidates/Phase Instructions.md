# Phase 3 — Central Question and Thesis Candidates

## Objective
Develop strong candidate formulations of the book’s central question and thesis while keeping them explicitly provisional until later evidence can confirm, weaken, or change them.

## What this phase must accomplish
Translate the clarified concept into a small number of precise, testable questions and thesis candidates. Distinguish between the author’s intended argument, historical propositions that require evidence, and possible interpretations that should remain open.

## Sub-phases

### Sub-phase 3.1 — Central Question Development
**Assigned to:** ChatGPT

**Work:** Read all completed Phase 1 and Phase 2 files. Develop several possible central questions that capture the real intellectual problem of the book without turning the answer into the question.

**Output:** `03.1 Central Questions.md`

### Sub-phase 3.2 — Thesis Candidate Development
**Assigned to:** ChatGPT

**Work:** Develop multiple candidate theses, including a strongest version, a cautious version, and a version that preserves the author’s idea while making fewer assumptions. For each, state what evidence would be required to support it.

**Output:** `03.2 Thesis Candidates.md`

### Sub-phase 3.3 — Thesis Stress Review
**Assigned to:** Claude

**Work:** Read the complete Phase 1–3 material and challenge each thesis candidate. Identify assumptions, overstatements, hidden causal claims, possible historical conflations, and what a serious critic could attack. Recommend which candidates deserve further development.

**Output:** `03.3 Thesis Stress Review.md`

### Sub-phase 3.4 — Working Thesis Selection
**Assigned to:** ChatGPT

**Work:** Use the Claude review and all previous material to select the best working formulation. The result is a working thesis, not a final historical conclusion.

**Output:** `03.4 Working Thesis.md`

## Agent instructions
All agents must read completed files from earlier phases before beginning. No thesis candidate may be treated as proven simply because it is rhetorically strong. The working thesis must remain open to revision after research.

## Completion condition
There is one clear working thesis and one central question, accompanied by known assumptions and evidence requirements.
