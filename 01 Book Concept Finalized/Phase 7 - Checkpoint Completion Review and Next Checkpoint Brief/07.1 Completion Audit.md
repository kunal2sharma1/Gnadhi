# 07.1 Completion Audit

## 1. Checkpoint Objective

Build a historically responsible and usable foundation for *The Final Walk of Glory* before the larger research program. The checkpoint must preserve the author's original idea, test it, define scope and terms, identify evidence needs, resist serious criticism, and produce one clear working concept.

The checkpoint objective has been met.

The remaining uncertainties concern the eventual historical demonstration of the working thesis, not failure to define the project. They should be carried forward rather than treated as defects in the checkpoint.
