# 07.3 Checkpoint 2 Design Brief

## 1. Purpose

Checkpoint 2 must turn the completed concept foundation into a research-ready book architecture without prematurely designing detailed chapters or assuming that every later finding will support the current thesis.

## 2. High-Level Objective

Build a coherent, evidence-driven architecture for *The Final Walk of Glory* that can connect:

**Gandhi as a living political actor in 1947–48 → assassination and its immediate political consequences → construction of the posthumous Gandhi → selected later inheritances, appropriations, rejections, neutralisations and counter-memories → evidence-based conclusion about what survived and what changed.**

## 3. Required Outcomes

Checkpoint 2 must produce:

1. A defensible historical arc.
2. A limited number of major parts that represent stages of the argument rather than a generic timeline.
3. A provisional chapter map with a clear argumentative function for every chapter.
4. A case-study framework for selecting only historically consequential post-1948 examples.
5. A claim-to-evidence architecture showing what each major argument needs to prove.
6. A source and citation strategy suitable for a serious historical book.
7. A clear separation between core argument, supporting context, and material that should be excluded or reserved for later research.
8. A structure that keeps Gandhi's final period, assassination, immediate aftermath and post-1948 political memory connected rather than treating them as separate books.

## 4. Scope Control

Checkpoint 2 must not become:

- a complete biography of Gandhi;
- a complete history of the RSS, Hindu Mahasabha or Hindutva;
- a complete history of Godse;
- a catalogue of every controversy about Gandhi;
- or a list of modern political events.

Later periods should enter only when they materially advance the central historical question.

## 5. Evidence Controls

The architecture must preserve:

- actor-specific attribution;
- primary-source priority for load-bearing claims;
- explicit treatment of source disagreement;
- no organisation-level responsibility without organisation-level evidence;
- no modern continuity claim without transmission evidence;
- no assumption that symbolic use equals philosophical inheritance;
- no assumption that criticism equals deliberate destruction.

## 6. Working Concept to Carry Forward

> **Gandhi's assassination ended his political agency, but it did not end the political life of his meaning. His violent death produced an extraordinary political and symbolic crisis through which the new Indian state, political leaders, and the public constructed particular meanings of Gandhi. Because Gandhi died while his political project remained unfinished, later actors inherited a fixed archive without the originating actor being able to revise, defend, reject, or redirect what they made of it. The decades that followed became a contest over which Gandhi would survive.**

This remains provisional and may be narrowed or revised by later evidence.

## 7. Central Design Principle

The architecture should be built around **historical mechanisms and turning points**, not around decades or a long list of political controversies.

## 8. Starting Context for Checkpoint 2

Checkpoint 2 may safely begin with:

- the final conceptual synthesis in Phase 6;
- the completion audit and lessons in Phase 7;
- the verified assassination/Godse research;
- the immediate post-assassination evidence;
- and the selected post-1948 case-study candidates identified during Phase 5.

Do not reopen broad discovery research unless a new architectural problem exposes a material evidence gap.

## 9. Completion Target

By the end of Checkpoint 2, the project should have a provisional manuscript-level architecture that can guide the next research and writing stages while remaining explicitly revisable.
