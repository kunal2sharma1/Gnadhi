# Phase 1 — Research Planning and Author Input

## Objective
Establish the starting context for Checkpoint 1 and prepare the exact questions that the research and GPT workers must answer.

## What this phase must accomplish
Capture the author's current concept without distortion, identify the key assumptions inside it, define the questions that need historical investigation, and create the research brief for the seven Claude accounts.

## Sub-phases

### 1.1 — Author Concept Capture
**Assigned to:** This ChatGPT account — Product Manager

Capture the author's raw thinking in a structured Markdown record. Do not turn opinions into facts.

**Output:** `01.1 Author Concept Record.md`

### 1.2 — Independent Concept Interpretation
**Assigned to:** GPT Worker 1

Read the author record and explain the concept independently: what the book appears to argue, what is unclear, what is interesting, and what assumptions require testing.

**Output:** `01.2 Independent Concept Interpretation.md`

### 1.3 — Assumption and Risk Map
**Assigned to:** GPT Worker 2

Identify historical claims, causal assumptions, political generalisations, loaded terminology, and areas where the original concept could become inaccurate or unfair.

**Output:** `01.3 Assumption and Risk Map.md`

### 1.4 — Research Question Design
**Assigned to:** GPT Worker 3

Turn the concept and risk map into the specific historical questions that must be researched before the book's thesis can be trusted.

**Output:** `01.4 Research Questions.md`

### 1.5 — Seven Claude Research Briefs
**Assigned to:** This ChatGPT account — Product Manager

Using all previous outputs, define seven narrow Claude assignments. Each brief must have one research question, precise scope, exclusions, required source types, expected findings, and a small enough workload to finish in one free session.

**Outputs:** `Claude Research Brief 1.md` through `Claude Research Brief 7.md`

## Completion condition
The author concept is documented, its risks are mapped, research questions are defined, and seven precise Claude research briefs are ready.
