# Checkpoint 16: Central Argument Stress-Tested

## Objective
Determine whether the book’s original thesis survives serious historical criticism and alternative explanations.

## What needs to be done
Give the thesis to an adversarial review process. Identify weak logic, overstatement, missing evidence, conflation, unsupported continuity, and strong counterarguments. Revise the thesis where necessary. The objective is not to protect the original idea but to make the strongest argument the evidence allows.
