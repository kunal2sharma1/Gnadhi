## Core Historical Rule

The architecture does not claim that the assassination caused all of Gandhi's later legacy.

The strongest assassination-specific claim is concentrated in the immediate 1948 transformation: violent death, political shock, mourning, state response, ritual and first posthumous construction.

Later inheritance, criticism, appropriation, counter-memory and continuity must be independently demonstrated.
