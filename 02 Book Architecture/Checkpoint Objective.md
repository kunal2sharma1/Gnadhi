# Checkpoint 2: Book Architecture

## Objective
Turn the locked working concept from Checkpoint 1 into a defensible full-book architecture.

The checkpoint must determine the book's historical arc, major parts, chapter logic, case-study strategy, evidence needs, and boundaries before manuscript drafting begins.

The architecture must serve the working concept:

> Gandhi's assassination ended his life as an active political actor, but it did not end the political struggle over what Gandhi meant. After his death, different actors preserved, criticised, appropriated, rejected, neutralised, or reinterpreted different parts of the Gandhi who could no longer speak for himself.

Central question:

> How did Gandhi's assassination transform Gandhi from a living political actor into a contested political inheritance?

## Architecture principles

1. The book is not a Gandhi biography.
2. The assassination is a historical hinge, not the entire subject.
3. Gandhi's final political position must establish the reference point for understanding what was lost when he died.
4. The book must distinguish living Gandhi from posthumous Gandhi.
5. "Legacy" must be broken into identifiable components rather than treated as one thing.
6. Post-1948 material must be selected through strong turning points and case studies, not an exhaustive chronology.
7. Modern examples must not be used to retroactively prove 1948 motives.
8. The book must include serious criticism of Gandhi and cannot become a defence brief.
9. Every major chapter must have a clear historical question and evidence burden.
10. No chapter should exist merely because an interesting fact is available; it must advance the central historical question.

## Completion standard

Checkpoint 2 is complete only when:

- the book's historical arc is fixed;
- the number and purpose of parts are fixed;
- chapter objectives are defined;
- the role of each major case study is defined;
- the evidence burden for each chapter is known;
- chapter dependencies and sequence are clear;
- major risks and gaps are documented;
- and the architecture can be handed to the manuscript-writing checkpoint.
