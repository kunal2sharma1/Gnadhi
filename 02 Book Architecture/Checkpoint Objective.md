# Checkpoint 2: Book Architecture

## Objective

Turn the completed Checkpoint 1 concept into a defensible **full-book architecture** before manuscript drafting begins.

The architecture must determine how the book will actually prove its central question, not merely arrange topics in chronological order.

The checkpoint must establish:

- the historical and argumentative arc;
- the major parts of the book;
- the purpose and sequence of chapters;
- the post-1948 case-study strategy;
- the evidence burden for each major argument;
- the distinction between core evidence, supporting context and material to exclude;
- and the structural safeguards needed to prevent the book becoming a biography, assassination dossier, political polemic or catalogue of Gandhi controversies.

## Starting Context

Checkpoint 1 is formally closed.

The authoritative starting point is:

`01 Book Concept Finalized/Phase 7 - Checkpoint Completion Review and Next Checkpoint Brief/07.3 Checkpoint 2 Design Brief.md`

The book's current working proposition is:

> **Gandhi's assassination ended his political agency, but it did not end the political life of his meaning. His violent death produced an extraordinary political and symbolic crisis through which the new Indian state, political leaders, and the public constructed particular meanings of Gandhi. Because Gandhi died while his political project remained unfinished, later actors inherited a fixed archive without the originating actor being able to revise, defend, reject, or redirect what they made of it. The decades that followed became a contest over which Gandhi would survive.**

This remains provisional. Architecture must not silently convert it into a proven final thesis.

## Central Architecture Question

> **How should the book be structured so that the reader can follow, test and evaluate the transformation from Gandhi as a living political actor to Gandhi as a contested posthumous political inheritance?**

## Architecture Principles

1. **The book is not a Gandhi biography.** Earlier life enters only where necessary to explain a later argument.
2. **The assassination is the historical hinge, not the whole book.**
3. **Gandhi's final political period must establish the baseline.** The reader must understand what the living Gandhi was still doing before the killing.
4. **The immediate aftermath matters.** The architecture must allow mourning, state response, ritual and the first construction of posthumous Gandhi to be examined as historical processes.
5. **Legacy is not one thing.** The architecture must distinguish symbolic survival, political philosophy, political practice, institutional influence, commemoration, appropriation, criticism, rejection, neutralisation and counter-memory.
6. **Post-1948 history must be selective.** Use turning points and case studies rather than decade-by-decade chronology.
7. **Modern material must earn its place.** Contemporary cases are included only when they materially illuminate the historical question.
8. **Continuity is a claim, not an assumption.** Modern similarities do not prove historical transmission.
9. **Gandhi's critics must be structurally integrated.** Criticism cannot be treated as an afterthought or as an attack that must be defeated.
10. **Every chapter must have an argumentative job.** Interesting facts are not sufficient reason for chapter inclusion.
11. **Every load-bearing chapter must have an evidence burden.** The architecture must identify what must actually be proven.
12. **Do not force symmetry.** Different historical mechanisms may require different amounts of space.
13. **Do not over-architect too early.** The structure is provisional until the stress-test phase is complete.

## Completion Standard

Checkpoint 2 is complete only when:

- one historical/argumentative arc is selected;
- the major parts are fixed provisionally;
- the chapter sequence and purpose are defined;
- each chapter has a central question and argumentative function;
- major case studies are selected provisionally and their role is explicit;
- evidence burdens are mapped to chapters/cases;
- chapter dependencies and transitions are clear;
- major structural risks are documented;
- the architecture has survived an independent adversarial stress test;
- and the final architecture is recorded as the authoritative handoff to the next checkpoint.

## What Checkpoint 2 Must Not Do

Do not:

- draft manuscript chapters;
- write polished literary prose;
- conduct unrestricted Gandhi biography research;
- build a complete history of the RSS, Hindu Mahasabha, Hindutva or Godse;
- include every criticism of Gandhi;
- include every modern controversy;
- lock exact chapter word counts before the structure is stress-tested;
- treat the architecture as final merely because it looks elegant.
