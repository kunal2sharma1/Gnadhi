# Checkpoint 2 — Phase Instructions

## Purpose
Design the full historical architecture of the book before manuscript drafting begins.

## Parallel operating rule
All three GPT workers in a parallel phase must receive the same completed baseline and perform independent work.

They must not read, wait for, or depend on another worker's output.

If a task requires a previous worker's result, that task is sequential and must be moved into a later sub-phase or handled in the same ChatGPT conversation after the prerequisite is complete.

## Phase 2.1 — Independent Historical Arc Design
Assigned to: GPT Worker 1

Determine the strongest chronological and causal arc for the book from Gandhi's final political period through the post-1948 struggle over his meaning.

Output: `02.1 Historical Arc Design.md`

## Phase 2.2 — Independent Part and Chapter Architecture
Assigned to: GPT Worker 2

Design an independent part/chapter structure that can sustain the working concept without becoming a biography or a catalogue of controversies.

Output: `02.2 Part and Chapter Architecture.md`

## Phase 2.3 — Independent Case-Study and Evidence Architecture
Assigned to: GPT Worker 3

Determine which post-1948 case studies, primary-source categories, and evidence types are necessary to prove the book's central argument, and identify what should be excluded.

Output: `02.3 Case Study and Evidence Architecture.md`

## Phase 2.4 — Architecture Reconciliation
Assigned to: Product Manager / this ChatGPT account

Read the three independent architecture outputs. Compare them, preserve the strongest elements, resolve contradictions, and produce one master architecture.

Output: `02.4 Master Book Architecture.md`

## Phase 2.5 — Architecture Stress Test
Assigned to: three GPT workers in parallel

Using the completed master architecture as the common baseline, independently attack its structure for historical overreach, repetition, weak transitions, unsupported case studies, presentism, and pacing problems.

No worker may depend on another worker's output in this phase.

## Phase 2.6 — Final Architecture Decision
Assigned to: Product Manager

Synthesize the stress tests and lock the architecture for manuscript preparation.

## Research rule
Do not conduct broad discovery research unless an architecture decision cannot be made without it. When a factual or historiographical question matters to architecture, perform targeted ChatGPT web research and record the result in the relevant architecture file.
