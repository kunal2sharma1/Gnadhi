# Checkpoint 3 — Next Checkpoint Design Brief

## 1. Purpose

Checkpoint 3 should convert the provisional manuscript architecture into a **research-ready chapter specification system** without prematurely writing the manuscript.

## 2. High-Level Objective

For each approved chapter, define exactly:

- what the chapter must prove;
- what historical questions it must answer;
- which claims are load-bearing;
- which primary sources are required;
- which scholarly debates matter;
- which cases are candidates;
- what counterevidence must be included;
- what the chapter must explicitly avoid claiming;
- and what evidence would justify merging, splitting, delaying or removing the chapter.

## 3. Starting Context

The next checkpoint begins from:

- the Checkpoint 1 concept and evidence foundation;
- the provisional five-part / sixteen-chapter architecture;
- the Phase 2.4 revision and architecture decision;
- the conditional status of Chapters 11, 13 and 15;
- the distinction between immediate assassination-specific effects and later independent mechanisms.

## 4. Required Outcomes

1. A chapter-by-chapter research specification.
2. A load-bearing claim inventory for every chapter.
3. A primary-source requirement map.
4. A scholarly-literature requirement map.
5. A case-study shortlist where cases are needed.
6. A counterevidence plan for every major argument.
7. A chapter admission/removal test.
8. A research priority system so the strongest evidence is obtained before lower-value material.

## 5. Scope Control

Checkpoint 3 must not become:

- manuscript drafting;
- broad unstructured Gandhi research;
- a complete bibliography of everything ever written on Gandhi;
- research into modern politics without a defined architectural purpose.

## 6. Special Priority

The highest research priority should be given to the questions that could still materially change the architecture:

1. pre-1948/post-1948 comparison of Gandhi's meaning and political position;
2. the strongest evidence for the assassination-specific immediate transformation;
3. substantive Gandhian inheritance suitable for Chapter 11;
4. a documentary appropriation/inheritance case suitable for Chapter 13;
5. one modern case capable of passing the strict continuity/inheritance test;
6. chapter-level redundancy or weak evidence that may reduce the 16-chapter count.

## 7. Completion Target

By the end of Checkpoint 3, every chapter should have a research specification strong enough that subsequent workers can execute research independently without ambiguity, while the manuscript itself remains unwritten.
