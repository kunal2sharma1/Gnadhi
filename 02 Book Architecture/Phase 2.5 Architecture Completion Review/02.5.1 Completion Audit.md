**PASS.**

**PASS.**

The immediate 1948 transformation provides the bridge between the living Gandhi and later inheritance.

## 4. Non-Negotiable Quality Controls

1. Do not claim assassination caused Gandhi's entire long-term legacy.
2. Do not treat loss of agency as sufficient explanation.
3. Do not treat symbolic survival as substantive survival.
4. Do not treat criticism as destruction.
5. Do not treat appropriation as proof of hypocrisy.
6. Do not treat organisational association as organisational responsibility.
7. Do not treat similarity as modern continuity.
8. Do not use present-day disputes as retroactive proof of 1948 motives.

## 5. Conditional Architecture Items

- Chapter 13 depends on a bounded documentary appropriation/inheritance case.
- Chapter 15 depends on a modern case passing the transmission/inheritance test.
