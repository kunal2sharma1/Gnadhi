Read `02 Book Architecture/Phase 2.2 Architecture Reconciliation/2.2 Master Book Architecture.md` and the authoritative Checkpoint 1 handoff.

Attack the architecture for:
- weak evidence burdens;
- cases that cannot support the claims assigned to them;
- insufficient primary-source requirements;
- unsupported generalisations from single cases;
- confusing symbolic survival with substantive survival;
- confusing criticism with destruction;
- confusing appropriation with hypocrisy;
- unsupported neutralisation claims;
- unsupported modern continuity;
- missing counterevidence;

1. claim or case affected;
2. evidence weakness;
3. severity;
4. evidence required;
5. structural fix.
