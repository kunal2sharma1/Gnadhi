# Phase 2.3 — Architecture Stress Test

## Objective
Attack the master architecture before it is locked for manuscript preparation.

## Parallel rule
Three GPT workers receive the same completed `2.2 Master Book Architecture.md` and the same Checkpoint 1 baseline. They work independently and must not read or depend on another worker's stress test.

## Worker 1 — Historical Integrity Stress Test
Attack chronology, causal transitions, periodisation, presentism and assassination-specific claims.

## Worker 2 — Argument, Reader and Pacing Stress Test
Attack chapter logic, redundancy, reader comprehension, narrative momentum, balance, and whether each chapter earns its place.

## Worker 3 — Evidence and Case-Study Stress Test
Attack evidence burden, case selection, unsupported generalisation, source gaps, and modern continuity assumptions.

## Product Manager after workers finish
Compare all three stress tests, revise the master architecture, then run final architecture decision and checkpoint completion review.

## Status
Ready for three parallel worker stress tests.
