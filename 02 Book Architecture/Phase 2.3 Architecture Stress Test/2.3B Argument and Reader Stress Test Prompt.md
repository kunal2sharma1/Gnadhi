# GPT Worker 2 — Argument, Reader and Pacing Stress Test

Read `02 Book Architecture/Phase 2.2 Architecture Reconciliation/2.2 Master Book Architecture.md` and the authoritative Checkpoint 1 handoff.

Work independently from Workers 1 and 3.

Attack the architecture for:
- chapter redundancy;
- weak chapter purpose;
- poor transitions;
- excessive front-loaded assassination material;
- weak bridge into post-1948 history;
- too many or too few chapters;
- reader confusion between motive, conspiracy and responsibility;
- generic “legacy” chapters;
- tokenistic treatment of Gandhi's critics;
- weak ending;
- pacing problems;
- unclear central argument.

For every problem provide:
1. exact location;
2. reader/argument consequence;
3. severity;
4. proposed fix.

Then test whether each chapter earns its existence and whether the Parts feel like stages of one argument.

Do not write manuscript prose.
Do not read other stress tests.
