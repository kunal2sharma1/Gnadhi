## Objective

For every provisional chapter, define exactly what the chapter must prove and why it belongs.

Do not draft prose and do not perform broad research. Use the existing evidence baseline to define research targets.

## Required Analysis

For each chapter provide:

- Working title
- Part
- Chapter purpose
- Central research question
- Load-bearing claims
- Secondary claims
- Causal mechanism, if any
- Required historical context
- Strongest opposing interpretation
- Evidence that would falsify or materially weaken the chapter
- What this chapter must establish before the next chapter
- What this chapter must not claim
- Admission status: core / conditional / merge candidate / remove candidate
- Research priority: critical / high / medium / low

## Cross-Chapter Checks

Identify:

- duplicated claims;
- chapters that can be merged;
- missing argumentative steps;
- chapters whose logic depends on future evidence;
- chapters that become unnecessary if a conditional case fails.

## Special Tests

### Assassination-specificity
Mark whether each claim is:
- death-general;
- assassination-specific;
- later independent.

### Long-term inheritance
Separate:
- symbolic survival;
- substantive inheritance;
- criticism;
- rejection;
- appropriation;
- neutralisation;
- counter-memory;
- continuity.

Do not treat these as interchangeable.
