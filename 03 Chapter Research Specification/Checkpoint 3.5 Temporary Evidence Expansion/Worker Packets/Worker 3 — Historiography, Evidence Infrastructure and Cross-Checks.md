You have TEN sequential tasks. Execute all ten continuously.

Do not ask the user for permission between tasks.
Do not modify the main concept, architecture, or red-team files.

## Core principle

This worker is not a generic research worker.

- comparing interpretations;
- identifying historiographical gaps;
- building reusable research matrices;
- testing claims against alternative explanations;
- improving traceability;
- detecting duplication and unsupported assumptions.
