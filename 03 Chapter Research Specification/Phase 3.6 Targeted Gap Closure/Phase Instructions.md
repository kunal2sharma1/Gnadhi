## Rules

- Do not restart broad Gandhi research.

- Preserve uncertainty.

- Completed research must be committed to GitHub.
- Temporary prompts must be deleted only after output verification.

## Completion Standard

A gap is closed only when the resulting evidence is strong enough to support a chapter decision or clearly demonstrates that the gap cannot be responsibly closed.
