# Phase 3.3 — Master Research Execution Plan

## Purpose

Convert the chapter research specifications into an ordered research program. This file does not write chapters. It determines what evidence should be researched first, which questions can run in parallel, which questions are sequentially dependent, and where research findings can still remove or restructure chapters.

## Research Principle

Do not research all sixteen chapters equally or simultaneously.

Research first the claims that can still change the architecture. Then research the foundational 1947–48 chapters. Then research later mechanism-specific chapters. Only after those gates are passed should lower-priority material be expanded.

## Tier 0 — Architecture-Critical Verification

These questions can still change chapter inclusion or the central causal argument:

1. Pre-1948 vs post-1948 comparison: what changed specifically after Gandhi's assassination?
2. Immediate assassination-specific transformation: what evidence distinguishes violent murder from ordinary posthumous mourning?
3. Chapter 11 gate: can substantive Gandhian inheritance be demonstrated through one or two primary-source-rich cases?
4. Chapter 13 gate: can selective inheritance/appropriation be demonstrated through a bounded documentary case?
5. Chapter 15 gate: can a modern case pass the strict continuity/inheritance test?
6. Chapter 7 vs Chapter 8 redundancy: is loss of agency sufficiently distinct from mourning/political shock to justify separate chapters?
7. Chapter 8 vs Chapter 9 distinction: does mourning produce a demonstrably distinct stage of posthumous construction?

## Tier 1 — Foundational Historical Record

After Tier 0, complete the primary-source backbone for Chapters 1–10:

- Gandhi final-period writings and speeches;
- January 1948 fast documentation;
- ₹55 crore government records;
- 29 January Lok Sevak Sangh draft;
- Godse statements;
- 20 January and 30 January criminal record;
- Patel/Nehru correspondence;
- trial and appellate record;
- immediate government resolutions;
- mourning/funeral and early institutionalisation evidence.

## Tier 2 — Long-Term Mechanism Cases

Research only selected cases that pass the admission criteria:

- state canonisation;
- substantive Gandhian practice;
- serious criticism;
- selective inheritance/appropriation;
- Godse counter-memory.

Each case must identify:

actor → version of Gandhi → transmission mechanism → historical evidence → consequence → counterevidence.

## Tier 3 — Contemporary Test

Research one modern case first. Do not collect a catalogue of modern controversies.

The case must be classified as:

- continuity;
- inheritance;
- appropriation;
- counter-memory;
- recurrence;
- analogy;
- unrelated.

Continuity requires documented transmission. Similarity alone is insufficient.

## Parallel Research Rules

### Can run in parallel

- Chapters 1 and 2 final-period evidence;
- Chapters 3 and 4 motive/conspiracy evidence;
- Chapter 5 organisation/responsibility evidence;
- Chapters 8 and 9 immediate aftermath evidence;
- candidate case research for Chapters 11 and 13, provided candidate cases are independent;
- candidate modern cases for Chapter 15, provided they are evaluated independently.

### Must be sequential

- Final pre/post comparison before making strong claims about assassination-specific transformation;
- Chapter 11 admission after substantive-inheritance evidence is reviewed;
- Chapter 13 admission after appropriation evidence is reviewed;
- Chapter 15 admission after continuity evidence is reviewed;
- final chapter count after all conditional chapter gates are tested.

## Evidence Standard

For every major claim, record:

1. claim;
2. exact source;
3. source type;
4. what the source actually establishes;
5. strongest contrary evidence;
6. confidence level;
7. whether the claim affects chapter inclusion.

## Stop Conditions

Research must stop and return to architecture review when:

- a chapter's core claim cannot be evidenced;
- a case is only symbolic and cannot demonstrate substantive mechanism;
- primary evidence contradicts the proposed chapter mechanism;
- the same mechanism is already established more efficiently elsewhere;
- a modern case cannot pass the continuity/inheritance test.
