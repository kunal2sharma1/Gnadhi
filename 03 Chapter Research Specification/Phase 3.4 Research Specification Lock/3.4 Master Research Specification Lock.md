# Product Manager — Master Chapter Research Specification Lock

## Status

Checkpoint 3 research specification is now consolidated into one authoritative framework.

## Core Rule

Every chapter must earn its place by proving a distinct historical mechanism. No chapter may exist merely because Gandhi appears in the material.

## Research Classification

Every major claim must be classified as one or more of:

- pre-existing before 1948;
- death-general;
- assassination-specific;
- nation-building/state-building;
- later-independent;
- documented inheritance;
- documented continuity;
- interpretation only.

The book must never silently convert one category into another.

## Universal Research Packet Requirements

Every chapter research packet must contain:

1. exact research question;
2. load-bearing claims;
3. primary sources required;
4. scholarly debates required;
5. candidate case(s);
6. counterevidence;
7. source-quality risks;
8. prohibited claims;
9. falsification or downgrade criteria;
10. final admission/merge/remove decision.

## Source Hierarchy

Primary sources are mandatory for load-bearing factual claims.

Scholarly literature is mandatory where causal interpretation or historiographical disagreement matters.

Popular/media material may locate cases but cannot independently carry a major historical conclusion.

## Conditional Chapter Rule

A conditional chapter is not protected by the original architecture. If the evidence gate fails, remove or compress the chapter. Do not manufacture evidence to preserve symmetry.

## Research Boundary

Do not conduct broad research merely to accumulate facts. Research is justified only when it can strengthen, weaken, qualify, merge, split, delay, or remove a chapter or major claim.
