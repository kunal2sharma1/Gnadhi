# Product Manager — Master Chapter Research Specification

## Decision
The three independent Phase 3.1 specifications converge strongly. The chapter research system is approved with tighter evidence gates and explicit merge/remove conditions.

## Global Research Rule
Every chapter must distinguish:

1. what happened;
2. what an actor said or believed;
3. what a court/inquiry found;
4. what scholars infer;
5. what the book argues from the assembled evidence.

Every post-1948 claim must also be classified as:

- pre-existing;
- death-general;
- assassination-specific;
- nation-building;
- later-independent;
- documented inheritance/continuity.

No chapter may attribute later developments to the assassination merely because they occur after it.

## Chapter Research Standards

### Chapters 1–6: Core Foundation
These chapters establish the living Gandhi, unfinished project, motive, conspiracy, responsibility boundaries and killing. They require the strongest primary-source discipline.

### Chapters 7–9: Immediate Transformation Core
These chapters must prove the transition from living political actor to posthumous political object. Chapter 7 cannot rely on the generic fact that death removes agency; Chapters 8–9 must carry the assassination-specific empirical burden.

### Chapter 10: State Canonisation
Retain only with evidence of durable institutional construction. A list of commemorative acts is insufficient. Require a before/after comparison and a defined institutional mechanism.

### Chapter 11: Substantive Gandhian Practice — GATED
Retain only if one or two primary-source-rich cases demonstrate transmission from Gandhi to later substantive practice. Self-identification as Gandhian is insufficient.

### Chapter 12: Criticism
Core. Use Ambedkar as the principal anchor and add other critics only when they demonstrate a distinct mechanism or intellectual challenge.

### Chapter 13: Selective Inheritance/Appropriation — GATED
Retain only if a bounded case identifies the exact Gandhi invoked, mechanism of transmission, selected elements, omitted/altered elements and political consequence. Do not infer bad faith.

### Chapter 14: Godse Counter-Memory
Core because it is structurally dependent on the assassination. Must remain evidence-driven and distinguish fringe memory from broader public significance.

### Chapter 15: Contemporary Test — GATED
Retain as a full chapter only if at least one modern case passes the strict transmission/inheritance test. Otherwise compress into an epilogue or Chapter 16.

### Chapter 16: Final Synthesis
Synthesis only. No new major historical case should be introduced here.

## Chapter Admission Test
Every chapter must answer:

1. What does this chapter prove?
2. What unique mechanism does it establish?
3. What primary evidence carries the claim?
4. What serious counterevidence must appear?
5. Why cannot another chapter do this job more efficiently?
6. What finding would require merging, delaying or removing the chapter?

If a chapter cannot answer all six, it is not research-ready.

## Highest-Value Unresolved Research Questions

1. What pre-1948 versus post-1948 evidence most clearly demonstrates a change in Gandhi's political meaning?
2. Which immediate 1948 responses are plausibly assassination-specific rather than simply death-general or nation-building?
3. Which single Gandhian movement/institution provides the strongest documentary proof of substantive inheritance?
4. Which single political case provides the strongest documentary proof of selective inheritance/appropriation?
5. Is there a contemporary case that passes the strict continuity/inheritance test?
6. Do Chapters 7–9 remain analytically distinct after detailed research, or should one be merged?
7. Does Chapter 10 demonstrate durable institutional canonisation or only commemoration?

## Research Output Standard
Subsequent research workers should not produce generic Gandhi background. Each research assignment must target a chapter claim, source gap, disputed interpretation, counterevidence requirement, or admission test.

Authoritative Phase 3.2 baseline for subsequent chapter research planning and execution.
